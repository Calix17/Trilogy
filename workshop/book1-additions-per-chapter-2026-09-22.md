# BOOK I — ADDITIONS PER CHAPTER (for v3 review)

*Every agreed insertion, in running order, mapped to the existing chapters. Structure only —
no prose yet. Review, cut, or move; then we render v3. Legend: [NEW]=new beat, [TEXTURE]=woven
into an existing scene, [REVISE]=change existing description, [PLANT]=one-line setup that pays
off later, [CUT]=remove something (here, a transition).*

**STANDING PRINCIPLE - Daniel's research (all three books):** always real physics on the line
where physics meets metaphysics - quantum mechanics, the nature of reality, extra dimensions,
quantum anomalies. This territory natively blurs "serious science" and "crank/delusion": the
same words serve both, so no one (characters or reader) can tell from the language whether he
was a misunderstood genius close to something or simply ill. His INSIGHT and his ILLNESS are the
same faculty (pattern-seeing is the gift; apophenia is the failure mode; they're neighbors) - so
"brilliant at seeing patterns some of us couldn't" is praise and first symptom in one sentence.
Even after the plot confirms he was RIGHT (the door, Laniakea), "genius or ill?" stays open -
right and unwell remain inseparable. NEVER let a character articulate this blur out loud (e.g.
"quantum mechanics is right on the line between science and metaphysics" - a real physicist would
wince; it kills the effect). It is ENVIRONMENT and REASONING only - enacted by people disagreeing
about him without resolution, notebooks read as either equations or raving - never stated.

**STANDING PRINCIPLE - Haven is almost-god but NOT omniscient (2026-09-22):** she can
instantiate a reality and has crossed ~millions of years, so readers first read her as godlike;
the revelation is her LIMITS. She doesn't know how the map/message arrived (and is intrigued);
she doesn't know if her own reality is real; she STOPPED searching and is at peace with vast
unknowns, because she already has a working model that lets the Arks live almost infinitely -
except the final frontier (the end of everything), which is why some Arks (Daniel's path) keep
seeking. Never explain the message's origin in Book I. Her accepted not-knowing is the content,
and it's exactly what Daniel cannot accept.

**OPEN NAMING TASKS (2026-09-22):**
- The STATION in the dream: a permuted "Enkidu" that sounds familiar/real (e.g. N.K.Do-ish);
  DROP the closed-restaurant idea - just the station name. It hints toward the INSTITUTE's name
  or a person, not the destination itself.
- The INSTITUTE needs a mysterious, symbolic name (TBD). The dream-name may point to it.
- DANIEL may want a name symbolizing endless seeking (options proposed 2026-09-22, TBD):
  Enosh (Hebrew "mortal man," a Genesis name evoking human frailty/mortality-seeking);
  Asa/Nod ("wandering," land east of Eden where Cain roams - the restless exile);
  Peleg ("division," when the earth/peoples were divided); or a Gilgamesh-adjacent coinage.
  Note the existing Enkidu/station echo - keep Daniel's name distinct from that.

**Through-line spine:** starts with the father -> finds Daniel's research notes on "reality"
(some genuinely concerning) -> the dreams begin -> the wrongness starts happening TO him -> he
doesn't stop (the pull > his fear) -> a dream hands him a name -> he wakes, it's real, it points
to the Institute -> the pieces connect. Grief -> obsession -> danger -> threshold.
