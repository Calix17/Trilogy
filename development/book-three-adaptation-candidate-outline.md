# Book III — compact candidate outline

24 September 2026. Arrangement proposal using the author's selected route. These are story beats, not committed chapter numbers. Sami as the main human anchor is recommended; Hesper's viewpoint and the wider cast remain open.

1. **A world to inhabit.** The colony reaches the planet carrying its different histories. Shelter works, but ordinary life beyond it has a cost. Plant the environmental pressure through daily work and a personal obligation.
2. **Haven leaves.** The departure ends an available form of support. Establish Sower's distinct presence, monitoring, and rescue capability without turning it into unlimited help. Daniel continues his investigation; the ultimate question remains alive.
3. **The changed and the unchanged.** Adaptation makes real lives possible. Others maintain Earth-adapted bodies in protected habitats. A family or public resource decision makes their disagreement consequential. The groups are not automatically identical to Earthborn and Woken.
4. **Haven's unforeseen descendants.** Elsewhere, Haven encounters the Ark built by a lineage from ancient mining organisms. Evidence connects the histories. The lineage predates Daniel; its people have their own purposes. This is an encounter, not a narrated survey of every seeded world.
5. **The deadline returns.** A sudden colony emergency exceeds what further adaptation or immediate environmental repair can accomplish. Let the reader understand the material limit and the changed stakes. Do not simply repeat Earth's same catastrophe or blame all danger on genetic change.
6. **Sower preserves.** It offers immediate survival in simulation and a later instantiated world. The changed people choose under pressure. Their identities remain theirs. Resolve the central human consequence through the anchor characters, including what survival costs them.
7. **The three worlds, a broader promise.** The agents' separate experiences lead to communicated agreement: preserve present conscious lives and seed suitable simple life for independent futures. Establish enough timing and contact to make that convergence possible. Medicine, adaptation, and individual departures remain options.
8. **Daniel's next door.** Earlier investigation leads to the empty Ark that found the answer. Daniel chooses its door. End at the choice or immediate crossing. No domestic coda follows under the current direction; the human resolution belongs before this scene.

Haven's encounter and Daniel's investigation can be interleaved with the colony beats. Their exact placement should depend on suspense and emotional continuity, not symmetry alone. Daniel's final Ark and Haven's inhabited lineage Ark remain distinct functions unless deliberately combined later.

The hazard, emergency, rescue limits, survivors, personal outcomes, and precise final image are unselected. This outline does not move the stone from Elias to Sami or restore Elias's retired second Vessel visit.
