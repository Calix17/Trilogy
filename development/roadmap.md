# Version 8 — suggested revision roadmap

This is a living proposal record while the author continues reading v7. No v8 prose is drafted or approved by this document. The current manuscript and reader PDF remain v7. Specific scenes, witnesses, wording and causal explanations below are options to assess together before revision. Implementation status is owned by [open items](open-items.md#version-8-roadmap).

## Purpose

Strengthen the early investigation and psychological suspense through discoveries that advance Elias's search while making him doubt his own interpretations. He may be uncovering what happened to Daniel, or repeating Daniel's apparent deterioration. The institutional, voluntary-admission, visionary and experimental-treatment readings are deliberately supported possibilities, not confirmed diagnoses or explanations. See [the author direction](decisions.md#author-clarification--institutional-and-treatment-misdirection).

The proposed progression is: active search → credible discovery → disturbing correspondence → self-doubt → secrecy → family consequences → Institute. Improve movement by replacing repetitive dead ends where possible, rather than simply adding length. The family material gives these discoveries their cost.

## V8-01 — A purposeful visit to Daniel's former town

**Author proposal:** Elias searches more actively for Daniel, follows the colleague connection and possibly visits the town where his father used to live. The trip produces a useful clue rather than only atmosphere.

**Suggested placement:** the investigation around current chapters 2–5. Consider replacing part of the repeated online agency trail with a visit, with one meaningful discovery and one disappointment. The town can remain ordinary while Elias grows increasingly suspicious. Destination, witness and exact sequence remain open; this does not require two separate colleague encounters.

## V8-02 — A colleague with an account Elias cannot comfortably interpret

**Author proposal:** someone suggests Daniel was close to an important discovery, while others were concerned about him or accused him of something. Elias may conclude that concern or criticism concealed an effort to stop the research.

**Suggested treatment:** retain the distinction between a promising result and proof of Daniel's larger conclusion. The same witness can describe worthwhile measurements, lack of sleep, missed appointments or increasing suspicion. Possible dialogue from the discussion: “There was something in the measurements. I never said there wasn't. But by then he wasn't sleeping.” This wording and the positive assessment are proposals, not current facts.

The accusation's subject and evidence are unselected. Do not invent a criminal allegation or clinical diagnosis merely to intensify the scene. Suppression, pursuit and capture are theories Elias may develop; no reliable narrator or independent scene confirms that the Institute abducted Daniel. Preserve the later encounter's voluntary choices unless the author deliberately revises that history.

## V8-03 — An overheard warning from an uncertain witness

**Latest author proposal:** someone nearby hears a conversation and later approaches Elias. The person could be a woman living on the street or another marginal figure whose information Elias does not know how to evaluate. She might say: “Don't believe what he said. He's also from the Institute. He works for the Institute.” Elias asks what the Institute is; she repeats the affiliation or gives an incomplete answer. The quoted wording is a scene option, not selected dialogue.

The warning could refer to the colleague, but the referent, the woman's identity, her actual knowledge, and whether the alleged affiliation is true remain open. Decide what she could have overheard and where. The existing colleague call occurs in Elias's parked car: an overhearing version needs a plausible revised location or encounter, not unexplained access to a private conversation.

**Editorial recommendation:** reuse or develop the existing chapter 5 woman near the parking entrance if practical, rather than add another cryptic messenger. Her housing situation alone does not establish unreliability. Uncertainty can come from an unverified claim, a missing explanation, inconsistent details, or Elias's own assumptions about her. She may know one true fact and infer the rest, or her allegation may be wrong; neither choice is settled. Give her an ordinary motive or activity so she is more than a clue-delivery device.

This warning is an allegation, not independent proof of conspiracy. If it introduces the Institute's name earlier, review the later private call: it must add something beyond repeating a name Elias already knows. Do not silently assign this woman to the anonymous map effort or establish a third faction.

## V8-04 — Office papers, old mail and the Institute address

**Latest author proposal:** Elias looks through documentation from Daniel's old office and finds letters, postcards, notes or Post-its pointing toward the Institute. This gives the warning a tangible connection and a reason to investigate further.

Choose one principal artifact and establish how Elias gains legitimate access. Daniel has been absent for twenty years: an unchanged, readily accessible office needs a reason. Archived office records, a box transferred when the office was cleared, or papers retained by a colleague are alternative staging options, not selected facts. Do not relocate the original notebook from Ruth's custody without deliberately revising its established discovery.

An old postal offer of help could echo the present campaign. Postal correspondence does not require a claim that the internet did not exist in Daniel's era. Date, sender, wording and address need enough consistency to support the chosen trail. A postcard or envelope might replace or simplify the property notice/lease route rather than duplicate it. If a handwritten note is used, distinguish Daniel's annotation from the institution's own words.

The warning followed by a genuine letter strengthens Elias's suspicion, but a letter from the Institute does not prove that the colleague works there or that Daniel was captured. Preserve that gap between evidence and inference.

## V8-05 — Daniel's notes mirror the present campaign

**Author proposal:** Elias finds remarks such as “They are looking for me,” “They already know who I am,” or language about not carrying something alone. The resemblance to his own advertisements makes him wonder whether the same people are now approaching him.

**Suggested restrained sequence:** “Another letter.” / “They know where I am.” / “They keep telling me I don't have to carry this alone.” These are candidate lines, not a required set. A few entries could record accurate outreach and Daniel's fearful interpretation at once. Establish whether they occur in the known notebook or a separate preserved paper; do not introduce a new diary by accident.

Avoid making every historical and present phrase an exact match. One distinctive echo can sustain the connection; repeated perfect matches could prematurely establish coordinated targeting. Keep the Institute's support campaign separate from the anonymous map/private call at writer level, even if Elias conflates them.

## V8-06 — Elias checks himself, then conceals the search

**Author proposal:** Elias feels as if he may be losing his mind, fears others will hear a conspiracy in his account, and increasingly conceals what he is doing. People around him notice concrete changes. Dreams, fatigue and apparently meaningful coincidences can still have ordinary explanations.

**Suggested actions:** he photographs wording to check his memory; almost shows Lena but loses confidence while explaining it; notices a resemblance between his behavior and the colleague's description of Daniel; or leaves Mara an incomplete explanation for a real work lapse. Select only actions that change a relationship, decision or understanding.

Let his confidence alternate: the correspondence is real, but his account of what it means may not be. Preserve his practical intelligence and occasional successful checking. Family concern need not prove that he is ill, and a real discovery need not validate every suspicion. No diagnosis, administered drug or compulsory explanation is selected.

## V8-07 — Preserve the existing emotional escalation

Use the new preparation to deepen the existing ant-song incident and missed recital. Do not automatically add another aggressive episode, another family rupture, or more dreams. Retain the three spaced dreams, Sami's control over sharing the recording, Lena's boundaries and independent commitments, Ruth's letter and agency, Daniel's real affection and chosen absence, and the quiet homecoming.

The two-room Vessel sequence, Haven's situated account and the institutional alternative remain. The cover's tree is symbolic, not a new literal room event. More external clues must not turn all care into a confirmed hostile operation.

## Assembly and verification before drafting

1. Continue collecting the author's reading notes here without modifying v7. Additions remain proposals until assembled into a coherent pass.
2. Choose one route through the town, colleague, warning and office papers. Resolve access, chronology, what each witness knows, and what each document actually proves. Check the existing car, woman and private call for redundant functions.
3. Map proposed replacements against current chapters 2–7. Preserve the original notebook's custody and separate the support campaign from the completing map sheet.
4. For each beat, write down the observed fact, Elias's interpretation, a plausible alternative and the consequence. A reader should have grounds for competing explanations; the narrator should not falsely certify either.
5. Draft only after the author is ready to turn the collected roadmap into v8. Review the practical choice questions already tracked as R-01/R-03 without exposing mechanisms that defeat the intended ambiguity.
6. On revision, render and verify source fidelity, chronology, clue/object custody and layout before each small commit and immediate push. Keep one current manuscript; preserve v7 through Git history rather than another live manuscript copy.
7. In a fresh reader test, ask what the Institute seemed to be, why Elias entered, when concern became suspicion, and where a clue felt earned or conveniently supplied. Ask for interpretations before suggesting treatment or conspiracy. Success does not require equal support for every reading, a diagnosis, or a cliffhanger at every chapter end.
