## Adoption rule

The author requests documentation of every substantive iteration, including tentative
ideas and editorial responses. Preserve the discussion source, label proposals and
decisions, and record integration status rather than silently replacing earlier routes.

When integrating this work into the bible or prose:

1. Apply explicit author decisions and check their consequences across all three books.
2. Keep unselected alternatives open until the author chooses a route; do not treat editorial recommendations as decisions.
3. Update `01-bible/bible.md` first.
4. Update character sheets if the decision changes motivation or history.
5. Revise the manuscript only after the canon documents agree.

The unedited discussion sources are retained under `99-archive/2026-09-session-notes/`,
including `author-clarifications-source-2026-09-22.md` and the later
`plot-clarifications-source-2026-09-22.md`.
